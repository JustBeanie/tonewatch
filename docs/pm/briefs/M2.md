## Required design (the PM reviews against this)

### Interfaces
- `dsp/spectrum.py`
  - `SpectrumFrame(t_end_s: float, freq_hz: float, level_dbfs: float, purity: float, tonal: bool)`
  - `class SpectrumAnalyzer(sample_rate=16000, window=4096, hop=1600, band=(250, 3000), purity_min=0.6, level_min_dbfs=-45)`
  - `feed(samples: NDArray[float32]) -> list[SpectrumFrame]` is **incremental**. Feeding a signal in chunks of any size (1, 333, 1600, 50000 samples) must yield frames identical to feeding it all at once. Write a property test for this.
- `dsp/segmenter.py`
  - `ToneSegment(freq_hz, start_s, end_s, mean_purity, closed: bool)`
  - `class Segmenter(tol_pct=1.0, max_dropout_frames=2)`
  - `feed(frames) -> SegmenterUpdate(opened, extended, closed)`. **Open segments must be visible**, because the matcher needs them for the early pre-alert.
- `dsp/matcher.py`
  - `Detection(toneset_id, detected_at_s, segments, early: bool)`
  - `class Matcher(tonesets: Sequence[ToneSet])`
  - `feed(update) -> list[Detection]`
  - Cooldown is tracked per tone set, by stream time (seconds of audio), **never wall clock**.
- `dsp/engine.py` (a facade)
  - `class DetectionEngine(tonesets, **tuning)`
  - `feed(samples) -> EngineOutput(frames, segments, detections)`
  - This is the only thing later milestones import.

### Algorithm rules
- Hann window. Peak search within the band. Parabolic interpolation on **log-magnitude** bins.
- **Purity** = energy in peak bin ±2 bins ÷ total band energy.
- Tone frequency tolerance is always a **percentage of the tone set's configured frequency**, not of the measured one.
- **Matching:** a tone set with sequence `[A, B]` matches when a segment within tol of A has duration in `[A.min_s, A.max_s]`, is followed within `max_gap_s` by a segment within tol of B, and B reaches `B.min_s`.
  - Emit the detection with `early=True` at that moment, while B is still open.
  - Do not emit again when B closes.
  - If B then exceeds `B.max_s`, **do not retract** the detection. Record the excess in the segment data.
- **Long tone** (a sequence of one tone) emits when `min_s` is reached.
- **Stacked pages** at the DSP level just means several detections for different tone sets in one stream. Merging is M4's job.
- **Timing resolution:** hop is 100 ms and window 256 ms. Duration estimates must be within ±150 ms of truth, so compensate for window smear and document the correction in a docstring.

### M2.1 Generator (`dsp/generator.py` + `tonewatch-gen` console script)
Composable builders:
- `tone(freq, dur, amp)`
- `silence(dur)`
- `white_noise`, `pink_noise`
- `voice_like(dur, seed)`: AM-modulated harmonic stacks with a 100–300 Hz fundamental that glides, plus formant-ish bandpass noise. It must **not** hold one pure tone for more than 150 ms.
- `add_noise(signal, snr_db, seed)`
- `clip(signal, level)`
- `phase_jump(signal, at_s)`
- `concat`, `jitter(dur, max_s, seed)`

All randomness is seeded. Output is float32 at 16 kHz.

### M2.5 Golden scenarios
Store scenarios in `backend/tests/golden/scenarios.py` as data: signal recipe, tone sets, expected detections (toneset id and detected_at ±0.3 s). **Do not commit WAVs.** Generate them at test time.

At least 25 scenarios, including:
- Clean two-tone (Motorola-like A 1.0 s, B 3.0 s)
- Long tone of 8 s
- SNR at 20, 10 and 6 dB (6 dB may be marked `xfail` with a documented reason, but only if analysis proves it)
- Frequency offset +1.4% (detected) and +3.5% (not detected)
- A too short by 40%
- B cut off before `min_s`
- Gap of 0.3 s (ok) and 0.9 s (fail)
- Two tone sets that share tone A
- Three stacked pages back-to-back
- Same page repeated inside its cooldown (1 detection) and after it (2)
- Voice-only for 10 minutes (0)
- Pink noise for 10 minutes (0)
- Clipping at 0.3 of full scale
- Phase jump mid-tone
- Adjacent tone sets 2% apart, which must pick the right one
- Tone immediately followed by voice

### M2.6 Property tests and benchmark
- **Hypothesis properties:**
  - Chunking invariance.
  - Any valid two-tone within 0.8×tol, durations within bounds and SNR ≥ 10 dB is detected exactly once.
  - Offset ≥ 2×tol is never detected.
- Keep the Hypothesis `deadline` and `max_examples` reasonable, so `just test` stays under 90 s total. Put long runs behind `@pytest.mark.slow`, run by `just test-slow`.
- **False positives:** 1 hour of mixed voice_like and pink noise gives 0 detections against 50 random tone sets. Mark it `slow`.
- **Benchmark:** `just bench` uses pytest-benchmark and runs 200 tone sets over 10 minutes of audio. Report the ×realtime factor in `docs/benchmarks.md`, with CPU model and Python version. The target is ≥20× realtime. If you miss it, profile and optimize (vectorize across tone sets, bucket tone sets by frequency) before reporting.

### M2.7 CLI
`tonewatch analyze FILE.wav --config config.yaml [--json]` resamples to 16 kHz when needed and prints:
- Frames (optional, `--frames`)
- Segments as a table
- Detections

The `--json` output is stable and documented, because the M5 API reuses it.
