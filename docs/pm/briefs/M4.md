# Brief: M4 Recording (M4.1–M4.4)

**From:** PM (Claude) · **To:** Codex engineer · **Model:** gpt-5.6-luna, medium

## Goal
Turn a call into a trimmed audio file: pre-roll from the ring buffer, post-roll until silence or the cap, tones cut out, and stacked pages merged into **one** recording per call. Encode it, persist a `Recording` row, emit `RecordingReady`, and enforce retention. Work test-first.

Before starting, read `AGENTS.md`, `PLAN.md` (Detection design → Recording and Stacked pages, M4), `docs/PROGRESS.md`, `backend/src/tonewatch/pipeline/channel.py` (the `RecorderHook`, `_OpenCall` and call grouping), `pipeline/ringbuffer.py`, `pipeline/persistence.py`, `dsp/engine.py` (`EngineOutput`, `ToneSegment`), `events.py`, `config/models.py` (`RecordingPolicy`), `settings.py`, `storage/models.py` (`Recording`), and `docs/decisions/0001-audio-libs.md` (PyAV encoders).

## In scope
M4.1, M4.2, M4.3, M4.4. No API, no alerts.

## Required design
- **Hook contract.** Today `RecorderHook(frame, ringbuffer)` can't see detections or tonal segments, so trimming is impossible. Extend the channel→recorder contract minimally: the hook (or a small `Recorder` protocol the channel calls) must receive the frame, the ring buffer, that chunk's `EngineOutput` (segments + detections), and the channel's open-call identity and lifecycle (opened / extended by a stacked page / closed). Keep M3's channel tests passing, and update them rather than delete them.
- **`recording/recorder.py` (M4.1, M4.2).** One `CallRecorder` per open call.
  - Start with `pre_roll_s` from the ring buffer, taken relative to the first tone of the call.
  - Keep appending frames until **all** of these hold since the last detection of the call:
    - `post_s` has elapsed, **or** audio has stayed below the silence threshold (−50 dBFS RMS over 100 ms windows) for `silence_stop_s` (early stop)
    - and the hard cap `max_s` is never exceeded
  - **Stacked page:** a detection joining the open call extends the post-roll from that detection.
  - **Tone trimming:** remove every closed or open tonal segment span, taken from `EngineOutput` segments in stream time, from the final audio. Pad each cut by a 50 ms guard and apply a 10 ms fade at each join to avoid clicks.
  - When a call involves several tone sets, use the **most generous** policy values: max `pre_roll_s`, `post_s`, `silence_stop_s` and `max_s`, and the union of `formats`.
- **`recording/encoder.py` (M4.3).**
  - Encode with PyAV to MP3, plus Opus/Ogg when configured, at mono 16 kHz, with a sensible bitrate (MP3 32 kbps, Opus 24 kbps).
  - Metadata tags: title `"<tone set names> <UTC ISO timestamp>"`, the tone set ids, the source id, and the call id.
  - Write atomically: a temp file in the same directory, then `os.replace`.
  - Path: `<recordings_root>/YYYY/MM/DD/<call_id>.<ext>`, where the date is the call's UTC start. `recordings_root` defaults to `<data_dir>/recordings`, and is `/media/tonewatch` when `settings.addon_mode` is set. Make it injectable for tests.
  - Encoding is CPU work, so run it through `asyncio.to_thread` and never on the event loop.
- **Persistence and events.** After encoding, publish `RecordingReady(call_id, path, format)` for each format, write `Recording` rows (duration_s and size_bytes from the real file), and publish `CallClosed(status="recorded")`. On encoding failure, `status="failed"` and the error is logged. Don't leave a partial file.
- **`recording/retention.py` (M4.4).**
  - `RetentionPolicy(max_age_days, max_total_bytes, max_count)`, where each field is optional. Add it to the settings model with defaults of 90 days, 5 GB and no count limit.
  - `enforce()` deletes the oldest recordings first until all limits hold, removes their `Recording` rows, and prunes empty date directories. It must never delete anything outside `recordings_root`: resolve paths and refuse symlinks that escape. Add a test for that.
  - Scheduling: a supervisor-managed daily task with an injectable clock and sleep that also runs once at startup.

## Tests (golden, the M4 done-when)
Build scenarios in `backend/tests/golden/test_recording.py` from `dsp/generator.py` recipes. Decode the output with PyAV to measure it.
1. **Single page with voice after:** the recording is pre-roll plus voice; tones are absent (no remaining frame with purity ≥ 0.6 at the page frequencies); duration = expected voice + pre-roll ± 0.3 s.
2. **Three stacked pages, then voice:** exactly **one** recording, all three tone sets on the call, tones trimmed, voice duration ±0.3 s.
3. **Silence early stop:** voice, then silence longer than `silence_stop_s`; the recording ends within 0.3 s of `last voice + silence_stop_s`.
4. **Max cap:** continuous voice longer than `max_s`; duration equals `max_s` ± 0.1 s.
5. **Opus + MP3:** both files exist, decode, and have equal duration ±0.05 s.
6. **Retention:** age, size and count limits each proven, plus the path-escape refusal.

Coverage: add `recording` to `backend/scripts/check_package_coverage.py` at ≥90%.
